Build with the collective.

We're a Rust & STEM open-source collective — software craftsmanship taken to perfection. Whether you want to ship a feature, mentor, or just learn in the open, there's a seat for you.

how it works
  1. 1
    Pick a project
    Browse the gallery or the experiments backlog. Apps, repositories and half-formed ideas are all fair game.
  2. 2
    Claim an issue
    Comment on the GitHub issue (or open one) so nobody duplicates the work, then say hi on Discord — that is where the day-to-day conversation happens.
  3. 3
    Ship it in the open
    Fork, branch, open a pull request. Small, reviewed increments beat big drops; CI and a maintainer will look at it quickly.
