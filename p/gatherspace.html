GatherSpace

● live

Spatial virtual office with proximity audio & video.

Log in as an avatar on a shared 2D canvas; audio and video connections form dynamically based on how close you are to others — just walk your avatar over to a group to talk, mimicking real office interactions.

WebRTC + Firebase power text chat, screen-sharing, draggable video tiles, custom presence statuses ("deep work", "brb") and a knock/ping to get a coworker's attention.
