Weather Voodoo

● live

Hyperlocal trip planner that scores the next 3 days 0–100.

Ask "what's the weather along the path from Phi Phi to Krabi at 14:00 tomorrow?" — it samples points along a real OSM route (ferry, hiking or cycling), fetches a forecast at each, and fuses wind, gust, rain, waves and visibility into a single 0–100 score.

Trip-window scoring ranks every start time by its worst hour, a live wind compass shows head- vs tail-wind for your heading, and GPS follow mode tracks you with a rotating arrow. Installs as a PWA and works offline.
