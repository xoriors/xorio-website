SyncOxiders syncs files and email across cloud providers and adds inter-cloud encryption, sharing and backup — an end-to-end encrypted layer over the storage you already use.

Written in Rust with a focus on safety and performance. One open feature looking for an owner: peer-to-peer file transfer in the browser.
